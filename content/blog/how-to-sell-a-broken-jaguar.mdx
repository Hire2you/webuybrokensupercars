Selling a broken Jaguar is not the same as selling a healthy one. Buyers who do not understand these cars will undervalue yours, and private sale can drag on for weeks with time-wasters and no-shows.

## Know what your Jaguar is actually worth

A blown engine, failed gearbox or electrical fault does not make your car worthless. Jaguars have strong parts demand and solid salvage value — especially popular models like the F-Pace, XF and XE.

General scrap buyers price on weight alone. A specialist buyer prices on model, spec, mileage and what can be recovered.

## Gather the basics before you enquire

Have these ready when you request a valuation:

- Registration number
- Mileage (approximate is fine if the dash is dead)
- Postcode for collection
- A clear description of what is wrong

Photos help, but they are not essential for an initial offer.
